the only difference within a pair is which indexes were listed, and in
what order.  So this says nothing about whether the rewriting works.  It
says what a program would have paid had the selector picked the other
configuration its file also pins -- the rule-ordering hazards of
Selector.v, with a number instead of a term equality.
'chosen' is what eval/SelectorTests.v pins as the selector's pick, so the
alternative winning a row would be a selector bug, not a caveat.
